## About me

I love software. I love building it, I love taking a hard problem apart until the solution shows up, and I love the bleeding edge: new languages, new frameworks, new ways of working. If something new comes out, chances are I have already tried it that weekend 🚀

And I love automation ⚙️ If I have to do something twice, I automate it the third time: business workflows as well as CI/CD pipelines. I started with Jenkins and today build pipelines with GitHub Actions and GitLab CI.

I studied MATSE (mathematical-technical software development) and hold a master's degree in **Information Systems Engineering**. I work at [@DatabayAG](https://github.com/DatabayAG) and have spent many years on [ILIAS](https://github.com/ILIAS-eLearning/ILIAS), the open-source learning management system.

Today I build AI agents and harnesses and connect them to real systems. My path started somewhere else: with chat systems, a Node.js chat server for ILIAS as my bachelor's thesis, and a WebRTC video-conferencing app as my master's thesis.

For more than ten years I have also trained apprentices in several IT professions. That taught me that writing code is the easy part. Knowing what *good* looks like is the hard part.

## 🧰 The right tool for the task

*If all you have is a hammer, everything looks like a nail.* I'd rather have the whole toolbox. I have worked across languages, stacks, paradigms and domains, from e-learning to data science to agent harnesses, and I never stopped learning.

> **A problem needs a new tool? You name it, I'll learn to master it.**

I don't stop at the tip of the iceberg 🧊 I study the whole thing. Using a tool is the start; understanding how it works inside is the goal. One example: I took apart Git's internals to understand how objects, trees and commits form the history, and why that history stays consistent. The same goes for the building blocks of every framework: dependency injection, autoloading, autowiring, routing, security and encryption. I know how they are built, not just how to configure them. That is what lets me pick the right tool, and fix it when it breaks.

## 🔨 What I'm working on

### 🔌 AI integrations on open standards

I connect AI to real systems through established protocols instead of one-off glue code: **MCP** for tools and context, **ACP** and **A2A** for agent-to-agent communication, and **AG-UI** for agent-driven user interfaces. I build agents and harnesses with my own SDKs and with public ones like Mastra, Symfony AI and Google ADK. I work with models from all major providers (Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, DeepSeek and more), and where data must stay in-house, I run **self-hosted open-source models** with vLLM and llama.cpp.

### 🧰 [tjstack](https://github.com/thojou/tjstack)

A skill library for Claude Code and Cursor. It gives coding agents an engineer's routine: read before editing, design before code, test first, verify on the real artifact. *Write less, but higher-quality code.*

### 🎓 An AI agent for apprentices: so the craft doesn't die out

Everyone talks about the skilled-worker shortage. Meanwhile, apprentices hand their tasks to AI and skip the part where they learn. I'm building an AI companion for apprentices that helps only with what they have already understood. **No delegating without understanding.** More soon 👀
